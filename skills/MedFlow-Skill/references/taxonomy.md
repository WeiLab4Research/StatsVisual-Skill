# Priority reference types and custom routing

The eight exemplar folders are priority reference entry points, not a limit on supported research directions or diagram structures. Select by research purpose and relations, not by the appearance or journal of a reference.

## Routing
- 01 covers simple and complex randomised-trial participant flow; record the actual trial subtypes. A trial-design schematic with a different purpose may use the custom route.
- 02 handles population selection, not model training. Use 07 when the principal question is the analysis or modelling workflow.
- 03 distinguishes records, reports and studies. 04 distinguishes index tests from reference standards; diagnostic evaluation is not prediction-model validation.
- 05 tracks changes between people, specimens, aliquots and assays with explicit units.
- 06 shows study events, interventions and follow-up. Do not treat visit spacing as proportional time unless defined.
- 07 separates statistical-analysis workflows from prediction development/validation. 08 separates conceptual relationships from input–activity–output–outcome logic.
- Combine priority and custom types in panels when appropriate. For an unlisted structure, use [custom design](custom-design.md), describe its actual name and purpose, complete the eight-dimensional proposal, and wait for confirmation. The absence of a local exemplar folder is not a rejection criterion.
