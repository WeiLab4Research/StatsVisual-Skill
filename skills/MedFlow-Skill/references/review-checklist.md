# Proportionate review and release

## Content and structure: required

- The current plan is confirmed. All relevant study nodes, branches, merges, conditions and counts are preserved; substantive simplification has explicit approval.
- Facts and count units trace to the materials. Unknown values are not treated as zero. Only justified same-unit partitions are added.
- Side exclusions connect at the relevant trunk transition; genuine forks and merges retain their study meaning.

## Appearance: visual judgement

- Text is readable and not clipped; borders do not crowd text. Boxes, labels and unrelated connectors do not collide.
- Main paths are easy to follow; peer widths and alignments are coordinated; branch whitespace is sufficient; there are no excessive empty connector corridors.
- The composition feels balanced at intended size. If no reference exists, review it against [independent layout](independent-layout.md), not an invented exemplar.
- No canvas title or explanatory annotation is added. Outer margins are small and clean, while internal spacing may be more generous.

One overall review is normally enough. Do not repeat measurements or exports to remove visually negligible numeric differences. Enlarge a difficult region only when a concrete defect needs investigation.

## Delivery: required

- Current SVG, PPTX and PDF files exist and open, with consistent study text and diagram elements. Allow small font-metric or page-dimension differences that do not impair the figure.
- SVG preserves live text, PPTX uses editable objects, and PDF is vector-based. Match page proportions approximately; a major mismatch warrants visual review, not automatic repeated resizing.
- If native Office visual rendering is unavailable, state that limitation. A program PASS is not proof of scientific correctness or aesthetic quality.
